# Privacy Policy

Effective: August 2, 2026

## 1. About this site

expedition33dle is a free, browser-based daily guessing game inspired by *Clair Obscur: Expedition 33*. No account, registration, login, or payment is required to play.

## 2. Information we do not collect

We do not request or collect your name, email address, mailing address, phone number, date of birth, payment information, government identification, or precise location data. No user account is created and no server-side profile is maintained linking activity to your identity.

## 3. Information collected automatically

When you visit the site, our infrastructure providers process standard server access information including your IP address, browser type, operating system, referrer, and the pages you visit: Cloudflare delivers and protects the site at the network edge, and Railway hosts the application. This processing is used to operate the site, prevent abuse, and maintain service reliability. We do not link this information to any persistent user identity on our servers.

If a server error occurs, technical details of the failing request — the URL, browser type, and the submitted guess — are sent to Sentry, our error-monitoring service, so we can diagnose and fix the fault; IP addresses and cookies are excluded from these reports.

## 4. Local storage on your device

The site uses your browser's localStorage to keep your in-game state: today's guesses, win/loss history, streak count, and game statistics. This stored state remains on your device, is never uploaded or linked to you, and you can clear it at any time through your browser settings. (Each guess you submit is sent to our server to compute the feedback for that guess, but it is not stored there and is not associated with any identity.)

## 5. Cookies and advertising

**Essential storage** — localStorage entries described in Section 4 are necessary for the game to function and are always active.

**Advertising (verification only)** — a Google AdSense script loads on every page while we verify the site with Google. No ads are served and no ad units exist on the site. Because your browser fetches the script from Google's servers, Google receives standard request information such as your IP address, and the script may set cookies or read device identifiers as part of Google's operation, under Google's own privacy policy. Advertising is planned: before any ads are served, consent controls will be in place for visitors in regions that require them.

**No analytics or tracking** — the site sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics or tracking scripts. Cloudflare may set strictly operational security cookies when protecting the site from automated abuse (for example, during a security challenge); these are not used for advertising or cross-site tracking.

## 6. Third-party services

The site relies on the following third parties:

- **Cloudflare** — content delivery, DNS, and security
- **Railway** — hosting and infrastructure
- **Sentry** — server-side error monitoring
- **Porkbun** — domain registration
- **Google AdSense** — site verification for planned advertising

Each of these providers has its own privacy policy and data handling practices that apply to data they process on their own infrastructure.

## 7. Legal bases for processing (GDPR / UK GDPR)

For visitors in the EU, EEA, or UK:

- **Essential site operation, security, error monitoring, and site verification** are processed under our legitimate interest in providing, securing, and maintaining the service

No processing on this site is currently based on consent. Consent will be requested before any advertising is served to visitors in regions that require it.

## 8. Data retention

We do not retain personal data on our servers. Game progress is stored locally in your browser and remains under your control. Server access information is retained by our infrastructure providers in accordance with their standard retention policies, and error reports are retained by Sentry on the same basis.

## 9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights regarding personal information:

- **Canada (PIPEDA)** — request access to personal information held about you; file a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada
- **EU / EEA / UK (GDPR)** — rights to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, portability, and objection; complaints may be filed with your national data protection authority
- **California (CCPA / CPRA)** — rights to know, delete, correct, and opt out of "sale" or "sharing" of personal information

To exercise any of these rights or ask a question about this policy, please contact us using the address in Section 12.

## 10. Children's privacy

expedition33dle is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this site, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

## 11. Security

The site is served exclusively over HTTPS, encrypted in transit. We follow data minimization principles — collecting only what is necessary to operate the service. Because no personal data is stored on our servers, the exposure surface for personal data is minimal.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the "Effective" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## 13. Contact

For privacy questions or data rights requests, please contact us at **contact@expedition33dle.com**.
